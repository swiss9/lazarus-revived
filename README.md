# ☥ Lazarus: Form Recovery

**Never lose anything you type again.**

Lazarus quietly saves every word you type into web forms — comments, emails, long posts, even half‑finished thoughts.  
If your browser crashes, a tab closes by accident, or you simply need something you wrote an hour ago, Lazarus brings it back with one click.

Originally a legendary browser extension loved by millions, Lazarus disappeared years ago.  
I brought it back. Better, safer, and free.

---

## ✨ What It Does

- **Auto‑saves everything you type** in text fields, textareas, and rich‑text editors (like Gmail, Reddit, Notion).
- **Works on any website** — blogs, forums, social media, online forms, web‑based email.
- **One‑click restore** from a tiny ☥ icon that appears right inside the field, or from the extension popup.
- **Full‑page resurrection** — restore every field on a page at once.
- **Version history** — browse older versions of what you typed (like a time machine).
- **Pin important drafts** so they’re never deleted.
- **Search your vault** across all sites.
- **Silence specific sites** — Lazarus won’t record anything on your bank, work portal, or any URL you exclude.
- **Smart privacy** — never saves passwords, credit card numbers, crypto seed phrases, OTP codes, or secret keys.
- **Optional PIN lock** to protect your saved texts from prying eyes.
- **Dark, modern design** that fits right into your browser.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Type anywhere.** Lazarus starts saving automatically (minimum 4 characters).
2. **If you lose your text**, click back into the same field. A small ☥ icon will appear at the bottom‑right corner. Click it, then click your saved text. It fills the field instantly with a gold glow.
3. **Or open the Lazarus popup** (click the extension icon in your toolbar). You’ll see all saved drafts for the current site, grouped by domain. Click “Resurrect” to restore any entry.
4. **Right‑click** inside a text field and choose “☥ Resurrect Last Text” to quickly paste your most recent draft.
5. **Press `Ctrl+Shift+L`** (or `Cmd+Shift+L` on Mac) to open the command palette — a fast search bar that finds any saved text on the page.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

- **Everything stays on your device.** No data is ever sent to a server.
- **Lazarus automatically skips sensitive fields** — passwords, credit card numbers, one‑time codes, crypto seeds, and payment forms are never saved.
- **You can blacklist any website** with one click. Lazarus won’t record anything on that site.
- **Add a PIN lock** to encrypt access to your saved texts. Nobody can read them without the PIN.

## 📦 Installation

### Firefox
1. Download the latest `.xpi` file from [Firefox Add‑ons](https://addons.mozilla.org) (coming soon!).
2. Or load the extension temporarily for testing:  
   - Open `about:debugging` in Firefox.  
   - Click “This Firefox” → “Load Temporary Add‑on”.  
   - Select the `manifest.json` file from the Lazarus folder.

### Chrome / Edge / Brave (Chromium‑based)
1. Download or clone the extension folder.
2. Go to `chrome://extensions`, enable “Developer mode”.
3. Click “Load unpacked” and select the Lazarus folder.

## 📜 A Little History

The original Lazarus was created in 2008 and became a must‑have tool for millions of users.  
It was abandoned around 2016 when browsers changed their extension APIs.  
People kept asking for it, posting in forums, and sharing memories.  

In 2026, I decided to bring it back — rewritten from the ground up for modern browsers, with a clean design, stronger privacy, and new power‑user features.  

The legendary Ankh icon returns. ☥
